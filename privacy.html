Privacy

This website collects nothing about you. If you email us, we use what you send only to reply.

1

What this website collects

Nothing directly. There are no forms, sign-ups, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and the fonts are served from this site rather than a third party.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use that data.

2

If you email us

When you write to contact@regulark.com we receive your name, email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Our email is provided by Zoho Mail and stored in Zoho's India data centre.

3

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to contact@regulark.com.

4

Changes to this page

If this page changes, the date below changes with it.
